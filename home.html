Our Story

Button & Bean was born and built, from ideation to operation, in just two weeks. All of this was possible because of our small teams love for coffee and community, and on the back of the belief that we can create something that makes life slightly more meaningful.

Our Coffee

We care deeply about both the way in which coffee is crafted and the way in which it is enjoyed with others. Button & Bean believes in taking a step back and enjoying the cup of coffee for what it is and with the people around you.
