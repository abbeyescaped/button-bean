Our Coffee

A small, diverse, and inclusive coffee company centred around the craft and experience of coffee. At Button & Bean, we embrace the slow bar philosophy: taking a moment to appreciate the process of making and enjoying coffee.

Our Mantra

We believe in the
slow bar philosophy

The slow bar is at the heart of Button & Bean. In a culture that often prioritizes speed and convenience, we want to create an intentional alternative in the coffee and service industry. Our drinks will be made with care, attention, and quality rather prioritizing quickness and efficiency.

Taking the time to prepare coffee thoughtfully.

Appreciating the craft behind each drink.

Using quality ingredients and techniques.

Creating an intentional customer experience.

Encouraging customers to slow down & appreciate the art.

Be present and live in the moment.

Intentional Brews

Specialising in
pour over coffee

Button & Bean specializes in pour-overs, a brewing method that highlights the flavor and depth of coffee. We stand by this brewing method thanks to the complete manual control, ensuring each cup of coffee is made precisely with care and craft.

The intentional care we put into craft makes this more than a cup of coffee; it’s a coffee experience.
