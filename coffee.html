Our Coffee

Our Mantra

We believe in the
slow bar philosophy

The slow bar is at the heart of Button & Bean. In a culture that often prioritizes speed and convenience, we want to create an intentional alternative in the coffee and service industry. Our drinks will be made with care, attention, and quality rather prioritizing quickness and efficiency.

Taking the time to prepare coffee thoughtfully.

Appreciating the craft behind each drink.

Using quality ingredients and techniques.

Creating an intentional customer experience.

Encouraging customers to slow down & appreciate the art.

Be present and live in the moment.
